## 📊 Viewing & Exporting Your Captured Leads

You have multiple ways to access all captured leads:

1. **In-Page Admin Drawer:** Click the floating database icon in the bottom-left corner of `index.html`, or press `Ctrl + Shift + L`.
2. **Dedicated Admin Dashboard:** Open [`admin.html`](file:///d:/antigravity/vastu%20landinng%20page/admin.html) directly in your browser.
3. **Export to CSV:** Click *"Download CSV"* to download an Excel-ready file containing names, phone numbers, emails, directions, and payment status.
4. **1-Click Call/WhatsApp:** Click on any lead's phone number to call directly or launch WhatsApp chat with them.
